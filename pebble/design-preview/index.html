Agent Remote on a Pebble Time 2

Four ways the watch can steer your agents. Screens are the real 200×228 pixels, painted with the 64-color cube. The watch has no Wi‑Fi; the phone carries HTTP.

Useful jobs on a wrist: notice that an agent needs you, decide, and speak a prompt while walking. Not a clone of Android or the LILYGO pager.

Right: Up, Select, Down. Left: Back. Orange or cyan glow is the RGB backlight. Keys A B C D jump to an option. Locked choice: D+B spec.

The hardware decides the product

Time 2 (emery): 1.5″ MIP, 200×228, 64 colors, four buttons plus touch, mic, speaker, RGB backlight, 128 KB app RAM, about 30 days if we do not poll. Bluetooth only. Dictation runs on the phone.

A · Pager

Notifications first. No typing. Smallest app.

The watch is a wrist beeper. The phone stays the real Agent Remote client. A pin lands when a turn blocks or finishes; you Allow, Deny, or Stop from the action bar.

Does
Timeline pins, three-card app (needs you / working / idle), backlight until you act.
Does not
Compose, transcript, watchface.
Ship
Smallest. Almost all logic lives on the phone. You cannot start a turn from the wrist.

B · Talk

Voice remote. One session at a time. Closest to the pager client.

Hold Select, speak, send. The current Focus session stays on screen: phase, elapsed, last line of the reply. Permissions and questions take over the display. This is compose without a keyboard.

Does
Dictation to continue. Focus walk with Up/Down. Allow/Deny. AskUserQuestion. Long-Select stops.
Does not
Scrollable history, new-session cwd, watchface.
Ship
Medium. Watch in C (or Alloy on emery). Phone JS chunks focus, jobs, continue, permission.

C · Desk

Always-on watchface. Glance, don’t open. Unique to MIP.

A color flag is the whole status language: grey idle, provider color working, orange blocked. Time stays huge. Select opens A or B. Minute ticks only; the phone wakes the face on job events.

Does
All-day glance. Timeline Quick View keeps the flag. Event-driven, not 1 Hz polling.
Does not
Act by itself. Allow and talk need an app or the phone.
Ship
Medium, battery-sensitive. The strongest Pebble idea, incomplete without A or B.

D · Pocket agent

Fuller Alloy app. Touch-scroll. Last messages. New session.

Closest to a real client on the wrist. Focus list, two plain-text bubbles, provider picker, then dictate. Touch pans; buttons still do the work. Live TUI, markdown, and attachments stay off.

Does
Everything in Talk, plus last two messages and new session (provider from /api/ping, cwd = daemon default).
Does not
Live TUI, markdown, attachments.
Ship
Highest. AppMessage chunking, more radio. Alloy makes it feasible; a C port to Duo is painful.

Side by side

These are not mutually exclusive. The interesting pair is Desk as the face and Talk as the app behind Select.

How the watch reaches the daemon

The watch never calls agentremoted itself. Config never stores provider identity; /api/ping stays the source of truth.

PKJS in the official Pebble app

Clay page for URL and token. Works on iOS and Android with no Agent Remote app changes. Polling, one or two daemons. Best v1.

Android Agent Remote as companion

PebbleKit inside the existing app. Reuses profiles, SSE, job watcher. Best live status. iOS waits — there is no ios/ tree in this repo.

Both

PKJS for everyone; if Android AR is installed it takes over the link.

Hardware cut

Time 2 first. Pebble 2 Duo (144×168, black and white, no touch) can get a reduced Talk app later. The color flag face stays Time 2-only.

Recommendation

B + C, Time 2 first, PKJS companion.

The face is what only Pebble can be. Voice is the only honest composer. Permissions and questions are the high-value “needs you” path and match every other client.
